# Roadmap — Luma

Open work only, in priority order.

- [ ] Confirm Samsung One UI crop, home/lock separation, Gallery handoff and overnight
      battery behavior on the physical S24 Ultra and S25. Emulator coverage cannot
      verify Samsung's private wallpaper picker or power management.
- [ ] Make an off-host backup of the signing key and its credentials; add the
      persistent wallpaper library to the household backup plan.
- [ ] Add separate home/lock playlists.
- [ ] Favorites & collections
- [ ] Wallpaper history - Easily find and restore a wallpaper you liked yesterday
- [ ] Manual override - Keep the current wallpaper for an hour, a day or indefinitely
- [ ] Battery awareness - Lower FPS or freeze animation when battery saver is enabled
- [ ] Wallpaper selection modes
Shuffle
Random wallpapers from selected collections, with configurable rotation intervals and no immediate repeats.

Time-based
Morning, afternoon, sunset, evening, and night collections. Ideally use actual sunrise and sunset rather than fixed clock times (except for work background collection or singles).

Weather-based
Clear, cloudy, rain, snow, fog, or thunderstorms. Match the mood rather than requiring the wallpaper to represent the weather literally. - met.no integration

Smart Mix - Combine time, weather, season, and personal preferences to pick suitable wallpapers.
